Use case: stylized-concept expansion board for an ORIGINAL 3D top-down action roguelite Steam indie game, working title “挖掘机拯救世界”. This is a visual design board, not a final asset and not a gameplay screenshot. Build a coherent humorous fantasy construction world: an earnest repair crew solves impossible disasters with absurdly overbuilt machines.

COMPOSITION: one wide landscape sheet, clean art-director presentation, 16:9. Use a strict four-band layout with large readable panels and generous warm off-white gutters. Upper-left 45%: hero gameplay vignette in an elevated three-quarter orthographic camera, showing a restored river-valley repair site and one active challenge. Upper-right 25%: three player-machine silhouette studies, each shown at the same scale and angle, with a small side profile showing its signature transformation. Lower-left 30%: six enemy design studies arranged as two rows of three, each with one clear combat pose and one tiny readable telegraph icon. Lower-right 40%: three environment challenge strips, each split vertically into BEFORE / DURING / AFTER; river valley, salt wind farm, and vertical mountain city. Include restrained editorial labels only if legible; images must carry the information without text.

PLAYER MACHINES: 1) WHALE-JAW RECLAIMER: mustard-yellow wide tracked base, dark petrol-blue chassis, small unchanged cabin, huge articulated whale-like bucket jaws, two violet magnetic coils; absorbs loose scrap and compresses it into repair bricks. 2) UMBRELLA MOLE: compact teal-and-coral drilling body, telescoping drill nose, deployable umbrella shield, burrowing posture, makes temporary tunnels and deflects hazards; keep it mechanically plausible and funny. 3) RAIL-SNAIL: long low turquoise machine with a snail-like spiral cable drum, magnetic rail layers, fold-out grappler arms, lays temporary rails and slingshots around obstacles; its silhouette must read as a machine first, animal joke second. Show upgrade transformations as larger negative spaces, extra joints, opened tool heads, and folding work platforms; never just scale the base vehicle.

ENEMIES: six distinct readable silhouettes with no pasted faces, no gore, no humanoid soldiers. 1) REVERSE-CRAB BARRICADE: sideways armored crawler with a huge reverse-warning light, telegraphed charge. 2) BOILER HIPPO: squat pressure-tank beast that inflates and vents hot gas, with a comically overbuilt exhaust stack. 3) DEADLINE EEL: thin segmented cable machine leaving conductive puddles and snapping between power poles. 4) DEMOLITION PIGEON DRONE: small construction drone carrying stolen repair markers, fast objective thief, wing-like metal plates. 5) CLAUSE COLOSSUS: tall folded-contract scrap golem made of stamped plates, breaks into readable shield panels and reforms; bureaucratic absurdity without readable text. 6) TRASHNADO: spinning tower of sorted junk, traffic cones and pipes orbiting a visible core, a moving area hazard. Each enemy needs one strong action pose, one obvious danger direction, and a simple coral/red or violet telegraph accent.

CHALLENGE SCENES: 1) FOLD THE RIVER: the player digs a channel, diverts a turquoise stream with temporary dams, and restores a crooked pump; before is dry ochre mud and blocked pipes, during is chaotic water and debris, after is a clean stream, grass clusters and oversized flowers. 2) WEATHER AFTER-SALES: a salt-flat wind farm with broken turbines and a ridiculous storm funnel; during, the player anchors towers while wind pushes scrap, after, turbines rotate and mint repair beacons glow. 3) CITY NEEDS A BREATH: a vertical mountain-city construction pit with stacked roads and vents; before is clogged, dark and rust-red, during is a clear repair route with enemy traffic, after is open blue air, lit windows, hanging gardens and safe shortcuts. Show gameplay space, not a floating diorama.

WORLD PALETTE AND STYLE: authored low-poly 3D, broad bevels, clean large forms, two or three value groups, matte painted steel, rubber, earth, water and plant materials. Use limited palette: mustard enamel #E9AD38, petrol shadow #253D48, bone #EEE3C7, danger coral #DF604E, magnetic violet #A486DB, repair turquoise #58B7AC, muted sage #7A8F6B, quiet ochre #C9A173. Warm key light, cool shaded sides, clear contact shadows, modest ambient occlusion, no photoreal noise. Big form 70%, functional secondary form 25%, tiny detail 5%. Preserve broad quiet ground masses and movement space. Humor comes from scale, machine body language, absurd engineering solutions and consequences, not meme text or faces.

REFERENCE TRANSLATION: use only the observed structural lessons from a local AssetRipper study of a commercial reference: shared color-block palette, stylized flat value groups, distinct module sockets, layered upgrade states, separate active/passive effect channels, and readable stage silhouettes. Do not copy any reference vehicle, creature, icon, texture, material, name, layout or logo. The board must feel original and suitable for Godot Mobile renderer, Blender 5.2.1 GLB production, and Godot-native particles/shaders.

AVOID: photorealism, generic sci-fi tanks, medieval castles, cute mascot faces, chibi anime, glossy toy plastic, dense greebles, realistic excavator catalog proportions, dark grim apocalypse, full-screen neon, cluttered UI, unreadable micro-details, random floating debris, gore, copied commercial game assets, and text-heavy infographic panels. Keep all silhouettes readable at 320px and from the top-down gameplay camera.
